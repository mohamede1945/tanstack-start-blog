## Quick personalization checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update site constants" icon="settings">
    Edit `src/consts.ts` with your title, description, and canonical URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the about page" icon="user">
    Replace `src/data/pages/about.mdx` with your bio, projects, and contact info.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace the demo posts" icon="book-open">
    Swap out the MDX examples with your real content when you are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frontmatter example

Every post lives in `src/data/posts` and starts with frontmatter:

```mdx title="src/data/posts/my-first-post.mdx" showLineNumbers
---
title: "My first post"
description: "What I learned building my first TanStack Start app."
publishDate: "2026-02-01"
tags:
  - tanstack-start
  - notes
---
```

<Callout variant="tip" title="Next step">
Run `npm run dev` and open the home page. You should see this post and the rest of the template
content.
</Callout>
